Our events

54

54 is a 3-day beginner-friendly no-code hackathon where students from different backgrounds form teams and launch successful startup ideas. Student teams are provided with mentorship, workshops, and networking opportunities to prepare for the final pitch comepetition in front of esteemed judges.

Forge

Forge is the premier student-run entrepreneurship conference in the Midwest. During the 2-day event weekend held in Chicago, guests and participants interact with one another through engaging networking opportunities, 1:1 mentoring, and more. Student startups also have the opportunity to pitch their ideas before judges to win prizes.
